## Notes on Filling LinkedIn Easy Apply Forms
Native `<select>` dropdowns in embedded Greenhouse/ATS forms inside the Easy Apply modal render unreliably with coordinate clicks (page/window scale shifts after each interaction). Reliable method: click the dropdown, then use arrow keys (Up/Down) to move to the desired option, then Escape or Return to confirm — verify the visible label after each change via a zoomed screenshot before moving to the next field. Applying via the full job page (linkedin.com/jobs/view/{id}/) works much more reliably than applying from the embedded search-results detail panel, where "About the job" and Easy Apply modals sometimes fail to load.

Lever-powered Easy Apply modals often render taller than the visible browser viewport, cutting off the Next/Submit button. Fix: drag the modal's own scrollbar (right edge of the modal, not the page) downward, or click a link/element near the bottom of the visible content and press Tab repeatedly — focus will auto-scroll to the next focusable element (often the Next/Submit button itself). Avoid double-clicking the Easy Apply button — a second click can land on the modal's close (X) and trigger a "Save this application?" prompt; click once, screenshot, then proceed.
